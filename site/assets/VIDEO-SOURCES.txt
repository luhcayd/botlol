Source URLs for the site videos (generated with Higgsfield, kling3_0_turbo,
5 seconds, 720x1280 vertical). These animate the matching portfolio images.

Download on your own machine, save into this assets/ folder with the target
filenames, then point the <source src="..."> attributes in index.html at the
local assets/ paths. Each video is used twice: once in the hero phone frames and
once in the portfolio grid. See ../README.md step 1.
