# Outreach System

This is the playbook for cold outreach. Follow it every time. The goal is that every email feels individually written and that no prospect ever feels like they got a template.

## Golden rules

- Never start an email talking about ourselves.
- Lead with how we help their business.
- Mention something specific about their product.
- Keep it conversational. Avoid long corporate paragraphs.
- Never sound desperate. Never sound salesy.
- Never use obvious AI wording.
- No em dashes.
- Always close with "Best regards," not "Best".

## The email structure

Every cold email follows this order:

1. Explain how we can help their business.
2. Mention something specific about their product.
3. Mention a few concrete video ideas.
4. Ask the soft close:

   > Would something like that be worth talking about?

5. Only after that, introduce ourselves using a rotating intro (below).
6. Then the one line explanation:

   > My name is Cayden, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

7. Close with "Best regards," and the name.

## Rotating introductions

Never reuse the same opening repeatedly. Rotate through these. Track which one was used per prospect so we do not repeat within the same brand or referral network.

**Variation 1**

Hello,

My name is Cayden, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

**Variation 2**

Hello,

I hope you're doing well! My name is Cayden, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

**Variation 3**

Hello,

I wanted to reach out because I recently came across your brand. My name is Cayden, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

**Variation 4**

Hello,

My name is Cayden. I recently found your brand while researching products that work well with short form content, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

**Variation 5**

Hello,

I recently discovered your brand and wanted to introduce myself. My name is Cayden, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

## Rotation tracking

Keep a simple log so intros never repeat within a network. Example:

| Date | Brand | Contact | Intro used | Status |
|------|-------|---------|------------|--------|
| 2026-07-13 | Ollie Smile | Savannah | Variation 4 | Replied, sent kit |

## What "specific about their product" means

Do the homework before writing. For each prospect, note:

- The one obvious problem the product solves
- One thing that stands out visually about the product
- The kind of buyer who would care

Then write the first two lines from that. If those two lines could be sent to any brand, rewrite them.

## Video ideas menu (pick 2 to 3 per email)

Use these as starting points, then tailor to the brand:

- A hook led problem to solution ad that opens on the exact frustration the product fixes
- A fast product demo that feels like a real customer filmed it
- A "why I switched" style testimonial angle
- A before and after that makes the benefit obvious in three seconds
- A comparison hook that positions the product against the annoying old way
- A three hook test pack so they can see which opening pulls best

## After the reply

When a prospect replies, move to the response scripts in `response-scripts.md`. For a warm reply that asks for portfolio, examples, or rates, send the sales kit (see `../ollie-smile/` for the reference build).
