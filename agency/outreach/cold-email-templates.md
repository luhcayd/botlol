# Cold Email Templates

These are ready to adapt. Each one follows the outreach structure: help them first, mention their product, give video ideas, ask the soft close, then introduce ourselves with a rotating intro.

Before sending, replace every bracketed part with something real and specific. If a line could be sent to any brand, rewrite it.

Reminder: no em dashes, close with "Best regards,".

---

## Template A: Oral care (uses Intro Variation 4)

Subject: a few video ideas for [Brand]

Hey [Name],

A lot of oral care brands are winning on paid social right now with videos that show the exact moment someone realizes their old routine was not cutting it. That angle tends to test really well because it hits a problem people already feel.

I was looking at [product] and there is a clear story there around [specific benefit, for example fresher breath that actually lasts or a routine that finally feels easy].

A few things I think could perform:

- A hook that opens on the frustration with regular [product category], then shows the switch
- A quick, real feeling demo of [product] in a morning routine
- A three hook test pack so you can see which opening pulls best in ads

Would something like that be worth talking about?

Hello,

My name is Cayden. I recently found your brand while researching products that work well with short form content, and I create short product videos for social media and paid advertising.

Best regards,
Cayden
