# Response Scripts

How to reply once a prospect responds. Same voice rules apply: conversational, not salesy, not desperate, no em dashes, close with "Best regards,".

---

## They asked for portfolio, examples, or a rate card

This is the warm reply. Send the sales kit. Reference build for Ollie Smile lives in `../ollie-smile/`.

Hey [Name],

Thanks for getting back to me, really glad this caught your eye.

I put together a quick spec concept for [Brand] so you can see the style with your own product, plus a couple of other examples and a simple rate card. Everything is below.

- Spec concept for [Brand]: [link]
- More examples: [link]
- Rate card: [attached or below]

If any of these feel like a fit, the easiest place to start is usually a small first batch so you can test a few hooks. Happy to tailor a package to what you are testing right now.

Would it help to hop on a quick call, or is email easier for you?

Best regards,
Cayden

---

## They asked "how much does it cost"

Give the tiers plainly. Do not over explain. Nudge toward volume for testing.

Hey [Name],

Happy to break it down. Right now it looks like this:

- Starter: 1 video for $100
- Growth: 3 videos for $250
- Scale: 5 videos for $400

Hook variations and extra revisions are $25 each if you want them.

Most brands that are actively testing go with Growth or Scale since it gives you a few creatives to run against each other. If you want, I can put together a package around whatever you are testing right now.

Would something like that work for you?

Best regards,
Cayden

---

## Price objection (too expensive right now)

Do not discount reflexively. Reframe around testing and start small.

Hey [Name],

Totally understand. The way I usually suggest starting is small, just one or two videos, so you can see how they perform before committing to more. That keeps it low risk on your end.

If it works, we scale up. If it does not, you have not put much on the line.

Want me to start with a single video so you can see the quality first?

Best regards,
Cayden

---

## "Send me more info" or "tell me more"

Do not send a wall of text. Send the kit and one clear next step.

Hey [Name],

For sure. Here is a quick spec concept I made for [Brand] so you can see the style with your product, plus a couple of examples and a simple rate card.

- Spec concept: [link]
- Examples: [link]
- Rate card: [below]

The short version of what I do: short product videos built like ads, not demos. Hook, problem, solution, benefits, call to action. The goal is to give you more creatives to test.

If it looks like a fit, want me to put together a small first batch?

Best regards,
Cayden

---

## They are interested but "need to check with the team"

Make it easy to say yes internally. Offer something concrete.

Hey [Name],

Makes sense. To make it easy to share internally, I can put together a short spec concept for [Brand] at no cost so the team can see exactly what it would look like with your product.

If they like it, we take it from there. If not, no harm done.

Want me to go ahead and make one?

Best regards,
Cayden

---

## They went quiet after showing interest

One light nudge. Never chase hard.

Hey [Name],

Just checking back in on this. No rush at all on my end. If now is not the right time, totally fine, just let me know and I will follow up down the road.

Would it help if I sent over one more example in the meantime?

Best regards,
Cayden

---

## They said no or not right now

Stay gracious. Leave the door open. These turn into clients later.

Hey [Name],

No worries at all, I appreciate you letting me know. If things change or you start testing new creative down the line, I would be glad to help.

Wishing you a great rest of your [week/quarter].

Best regards,
Cayden
