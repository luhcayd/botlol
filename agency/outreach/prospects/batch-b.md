# Reelo Prospect Research — Batch B

**Verticals covered:** (1) Kitchen gadgets & cookware, (2) Home & cleaning products, (3) Beauty, grooming & lifestyle gadgets.

**Summary:** 26 prospects included — by confidence: 19 HIGH, 7 MEDIUM. By vertical: Kitchen & cookware 6, Home & cleaning 8, Beauty/grooming/lifestyle 12.

**Researched but EXCLUDED** (no verifiable public email OR contact form, or disqualified): Truman's (acquired by Henkel; no verified contact), Great Jones (acquired by Meyer Corp — giant; no verified contact), Skin Gym (no verified email/form found), Bravo Sierra (email redacted in all sources; no confirmed contact URL), Kitchy Gadgets (no verified contact), Force of Nature (emails redacted; no confirmed contact URL), YouCopia (no verified contact), Canopy (getcanopy.co — disqualified: already leans heavily on influencer/creator collaborations), Solawave (disqualified: over-indexes on UGC/creator ads, scaling into Target/Ulta), Jaspr (team@jaspr.co verified but disqualified: core marketing already built on influencer/creator partnerships).

**Verification note:** Every email below was captured from a search-result snippet/summary that surfaced the address alongside the cited page (usually the brand's own contact/help/FAQ page). No addresses were guessed or pattern-inferred. HIGH = address (or form) tied to the brand's own site page; MEDIUM = address surfaced primarily via a third-party directory (RocketReach/LeadIQ/ZoomInfo/Trustpilot) or a related/older page.
