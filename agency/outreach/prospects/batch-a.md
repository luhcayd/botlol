# Reelo Outreach Prospects — Batch A

**Verticals covered:** (1) Pet products, (2) Oral care, (3) Health & supplements
**Researcher note:** All emails/contact URLs below were seen in web search results that cited the brand's own contact/press/affiliate pages. WebFetch was blocked by the sandbox proxy (HTTP 403), so verification relied on search-result synthesis over brand-owned pages; the cited source URLs themselves appeared as live search-result links. No email was invented or pattern-guessed — brands where only an email *format* (e.g. "first@domain") was reported, with no concrete published address or confirmed contact form, were excluded.

## Summary
- **Total included:** 29 prospects
- **By vertical:** Pet products = 11 | Oral care = 8 | Health & supplements = 10
- **By confidence:** HIGH (published email on brand's own page) = 22 | MEDIUM / contact-form-only (no published email captured, or email from directory) = 7
- **Researched but EXCLUDED for lacking verifiable contact / poor fit:** Shyn (no published email or confirmed contact form found), Beam / shopbeam.com (no published email or contact form found), Goby (email masked in results, unverified), Cali White (only a Refersion affiliate signup, no published email/contact page), Snif (search conflated it — snif.co is a fragrance brand, not pet grooming), Only Natural Pet (broad multi-brand marketplace, contact form only — weaker single-product fit). Excluded as too large / already heavy on video ads or celebrity-influencer marketing: Grüns (acquired by Unilever, 2026), Jinx (Chris Evans + MrBeast + Tinx partnerships already), Dr. Marty (large direct-response video advertiser). Excluded as non-US focus: Perfora (Gurugram, India; India phone/WhatsApp only).
