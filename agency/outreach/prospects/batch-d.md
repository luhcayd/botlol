# Reelo Prospect Research — Batch D

**Verticals covered:** (1) Kitchen gadgets & cookware, (2) Home & cleaning products, (3) Beauty, grooming & lifestyle gadgets.

**Summary:** 23 fully-verified prospects included — by confidence: 18 HIGH, 5 MEDIUM. By vertical: Kitchen & cookware 7, Home & cleaning 7, Beauty/grooming/lifestyle 9. All 23 are new (no overlap with Batch A, Batch B, or the already-contacted list).

**Verification note:** Every email below was captured from a search-result snippet that surfaced the address alongside the cited brand-owned page (usually the Contact / Help / FAQ / Press page). No address was guessed or pattern-inferred. WebFetch was proxy-blocked, so verification relied on search-result synthesis over brand-owned URLs. HIGH = address (or form) tied to the brand's own site page; MEDIUM = form-only (no email captured) or address surfaced via a portfolio/parent domain. Where a snippet masked the actual address (shown as "[email protected]"), it was NOT recorded as an email — a confirmed on-site form/contact URL is listed instead.
