# Reelo Prospect Research — Batch C

**Verticals covered:** (1) Pet products, (2) Oral care, (3) Health & supplements. All brands checked against Batch A, Batch B, and the already-contacted list — **no duplicates**.

**Summary:** 23 prospects included — by confidence: **16 HIGH, 7 MEDIUM**. By vertical: **Pet products 8, Oral care 6, Health & supplements 9.** **Researched but EXCLUDED** for no verifiable contact or poor fit: BARK Bright (part of BARK — giant), Snow Oral Care (already leans heavily on creator/celebrity ambassador ads), GuruNanda (too large / already viral on Amazon), Twice (celebrity-founded by Lenny Kravitz, no contact captured), By Humankind (no published email or contact form surfaced), Bears with Benefits (Europe-focused; only a UK support email), Just Thrive (no published email or form surfaced), Earth Rated (Montréal HQ / distributor-only model, and runs an ambassador program), and the obvious giants (Seed, HUM, Goli, Momentous, Bloom, Grüns, Zesty Paws).

**Verification note:** Every email below was captured from a search-result snippet/summary that surfaced the address alongside the cited brand-owned page (contact/FAQ/press/wholesale). No address was invented or pattern-inferred — brands where sources showed only an email *format* (e.g. "first@domain") with no concrete published address were recorded as **contact-form-only**. HIGH = published email tied to the brand's own page. MEDIUM = contact-form-only (no published email captured) or a very small operation.
