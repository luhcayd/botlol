## Voice rules (apply everywhere client facing)

- Never start an email talking about ourselves. Help them first.
- Mention something specific about their product.
- Conversational, not salesy, never desperate.
- No obvious AI wording.
- No em dashes.
- Always close with "Best regards,".
- Rotate intros so no opening repeats within a network.
- Spec work is always labeled "Spec Concept." Never imply a company hired us if they did not.
- Do not claim to be a Meta Ads expert until we have real client experience.
