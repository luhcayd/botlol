## 1. What we actually are

We are a premium creative agency that produces AI generated UGC and paid social ad creatives for ecommerce brands.

We are not a generic UGC creator, and we do not want to be positioned as one. The long term goal is a real creative studio that brands hire for creative testing, Meta ad creatives, and eventually creative strategy and ad management.

The one line version:

> Performance creative for product brands. We help brands test more creatives and scale winning ads.

## 2. The core belief

We are not selling videos. We are selling outcomes:

- More creatives in rotation
- More testing
- More winning ads
- Better performance

The video is the product. The value is helping brands test more and win more. Every message, page, and spec ad should reflect that.

## 3. Who we sell to (ICP)

- Not a Fortune 500 company

- Wants more creative testing

Ideal industries:

- Oral care
- Pet products

- Cleaning products
- Home products

Avoid:

- Tea companies
- Restaurants

## 6. Video style and script framework

Videos should feel like advertisements, not demos. Never just show someone using a product. Sell why the viewer should care.

Every script follows:

1. Hook
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Benefits
5. Call to action

Tone: authentic, creator style, natural, not overproduced. It should feel like a real person, not a commercial set.

## 7. Production workflow (AI stack)

- Primary generation: Higgsfield
- Draft with cheaper models first, then re render finals with higher quality models
- Edit in CapCut
- AI speeds up production. It does not replace strategy.

Standard production flow for a spec or client video:

1. Research the brand and product. Find the obvious problem it solves.
2. Write the script using the Hook to CTA framework.
3. Generate draft visuals on a cheap model to lock the concept.
4. Re render final visuals on a higher quality model.
5. Add voiceover and captions.
6. Edit in CapCut for pacing and hook strength.
7. Deliver with clear labeling. Spec work is always labeled "Spec Concept".

## 8. Sales process

1. Find brands that fit the ICP.
2. Build a specific spec ad or a specific set of video ideas for that brand.
3. Send a cold email using the outreach system (see `outreach/`).
4. When they reply, send portfolio, examples, and rate card.
5. Close a Starter or Growth first project.
6. Overdeliver, then move them to a repeat package.

Every email should feel individually written. Every spec video should be built for that specific brand. The goal is for prospects to think "this feels like a real creative studio," not "this is another AI creator."

## 10. Three year roadmap

Year 1: Foundation

- Build a portfolio of strong spec ads across 3 to 4 ideal industries
- Land first 3 to 5 paying clients
- Turn one strong result into the first mini case study
- Ship the premium website

Year 2: Systems and repeat revenue

- Move clients to monthly creative packages
- Bring on one or two editors
- Start offering Meta creative testing once we have real experience
- Build repeatable production and delivery systems

Year 3: Agency

- Recurring monthly retainers as the core of revenue
- Small team of editors and a creative process
- Meta creative strategy as a real service
- Performance based case studies
- Begin working with nationally recognized ecommerce brands

## 11. Operating principles

- Quality over quantity in every part of the business
- Never sound desperate, never sound salesy
- Never use obvious AI wording in client facing copy
- No em dashes in outreach
- Always close emails with "Best regards,"
- Be honest about what is spec and what is real
- Do not claim expertise we have not earned yet
